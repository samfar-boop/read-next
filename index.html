Data: goodbooks-10k, about 6 million Goodreads ratings from 53,000 readers, collected around 2017, so newer books are missing. Of its 10,000 books, 469 explicit adult titles were left out, and 113 popular children's books it was missing were added by hand (marked "Added by hand"); those have no reader ratings, so they are matched on theme and age. Links and matches come from those ratings; shelves and age bands come from readers' own tags.
