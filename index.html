Age bands are estimates from how thousands of Goodreads readers tagged each book (picture book, middle grade, young adult, school reading and so on). They are a starting point, not a content rating. Check any book you're unsure about at Common Sense Media before handing it over.

Data: goodbooks-10k, about 6 million Goodreads ratings from 53,000 readers, collected around 2017, so newer books are missing. Of its 10,000 books, 469 explicit adult titles were left out. Links and matches come from those ratings; shelves and age bands come from readers' own tags.
